Taking the sleeper train to a conference always feels more humane than flying. The steady vibration of the bogies, the intermittent glare of signal lights sliding along the rain-streaked window, and the absence of cellular reception create a cocoon for uninterrupted reading.

I spent most of the night with a draft on topological quantum memory. By the time we crossed into Switzerland at dawn, the fog was lifting over the pine valleys.
