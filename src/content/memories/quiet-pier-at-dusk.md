After five days of nonstop presentations and workshop arguments, three of us caught a local bus out to the coast.

The sea was completely flat, the color of wet zinc. We stood at the end of the wooden pier for an hour without saying much, just watching gulls circle the breakwater. It was the first time in months my head felt genuinely clear.
