The campus was entirely deserted when I arrived before sunrise. Heavy wet snow had dampened all sound from the avenue. Footprints from a single groundskeeper led toward the heating plant, but otherwise the paths were untouched.

Walking through the stone cloisters with cold hands and hot tea in a thermos is one of my clearest memories from the first year of my doctoral studies.
